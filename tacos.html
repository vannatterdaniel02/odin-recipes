Tacos

Description

These homemade tacos are easy tp prepare and packed with flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 packet of taco seasoning
  • 8 taco shells
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 tomato
  • Sour Cream

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beed in a skillet
  2. Drain off excess grease.
  3. Add the taco seasoning and water according to the package directions.
  4. Simmer the beef until the sauce thickens.
  5. Warm the taco shells.
  6. Fill each shell with seasoned beef.
  7. Add lettuce, cheese, tomato, and sour cream.
  8. Serve immediatley.
