Meatballs

Description

These homemade meatballs are tender, flavorful, and easy to prepare. They are made with ground beef, breadcrumbs, seasonings, and other simple ingredients.

Serve these meatballs with your favorite pasta and sauce

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cloves Garlic, mince
  • 1 teaspoon Italian Seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup marinara sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F.
  2. Combine the ground beef, breadcrumbs, Parmasan cheese, egg, garlic, and seasoning in a bowl
  3. Mix the ingredients until everything is well combined.
  4. Shape the mixture into evenly sized meatballs.
  5. Place the meatballs on a baking sheet and bake for 20 minutes, or until cooked through.
  6. Heat the marinara sauce in saucepan.
  7. Add the cooked meatballs to the sauce and serve.
