Spaghetti

Description

Spaghetti is a classic Italian-inspired dish made with pasta and a flavorful tomato sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound Spaghetti
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 clove garlic
  • 1 teaspoon Itailian Seasoning

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot.
  2. Cook the spaghetti according to package directions
  3. Brown the ground beef in a skillet
  4. Add the onion and garlic and cook until softened
  5. Add the marinara sauce and Italian seasoning
  6. Simmer the sauce for several minutes
  7. Drain spaghetti.
  8. Serve the spaghetto with the meat sauce
